QuickBite Product Backlog Prioritization

1. Overview

As the Product Owner, I organized the Product Backlog based on which features are most important to customers, how the features depend on each other, and what our team needs to build first.

Our main goal is to develop the basic ordering process before moving on to additional features. We also considered the amount of work involved and how each feature contributes to the overall application.

2. Top Five Product Backlog Items

Priority 1: PBI-01 Display Restaurant Listings

I placed this feature first because customers need to see which restaurants are available before they can do anything else on the platform. It is the starting point of the ordering process and allows customers to choose where they want to order from.

Priority 2: PBI-02 View Restaurant Menu

Once a customer selects a restaurant, the next step is to view its menu. Customers need to know what food is available and how much it costs before deciding what to order. That's why this feature comes right after restaurant listings.

Priority 3: PBI-03 Add Items to Shopping Cart

After browsing the menu, customers need a way to select the food they want. The shopping cart allows them to collect their chosen items before placing an order. We prioritized this feature because it is an important part of the ordering process.

Priority 4: PBI-06 Select Pickup Time

Since QuickBite focuses on restaurant pickup orders, customers should be able to choose when they want to collect their food. I placed this feature before order placement because the pickup time needs to be included when the customer submits an order.

Priority 5: PBI-05 Place a Pickup Order

This is one of the most important features because it allows customers to actually submit their orders. However, it depends on the previous features being available, such as selecting food items and choosing a pickup time. That's why I placed it fifth, even though it is a key part of the application.

3. Remaining Product Backlog Items

The remaining features are also important for QuickBite to function properly.

For example, customers need an order confirmation to know that their order was placed successfully. They should also be able to track their order status to know when their food is ready.

On the restaurant side, employees need to view incoming orders and update their statuses so they can manage food preparation and pickups.

We also included the ability to remove items from the shopping cart, allowing customers to make changes before ordering.

Although these features are important, we decided to focus first on the main ordering process. The remaining items can be selected based on our Sprint capacity, development progress, and feedback.

4. Prioritization Approach

When deciding the backlog order, we mainly considered three factors.

Customer Value: We considered which features would be most useful to customers and help them use the platform effectively.

Feature Dependencies: We looked at which features needed to be implemented before others could work properly.

Development Effort: We considered how much work each feature would require and what our team could realistically complete.

We understand that priorities may change as development progresses. If we receive feedback or new requirements, we can review the backlog and adjust the order when necessary.
